About

A bit about my background

I'm currently in my 7th semester of a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology, with my focus shifting steadily toward Machine Learning and applied AI. Python is the language I work in most, and I've spent the past stretch of my studies building a practical foundation in data science — from cleaning messy datasets to building models that make sense of them.

My project work has mostly involved the core ML pipeline: data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, model building, and evaluation. I try to understand why a step matters, not just how to call the function for it, which is why I spend time on things like outlier analysis and cross-validation rather than skipping straight to a final score.

Outside of ML specifically, I also have experience in Android development and databases, which has been useful for understanding how models eventually fit into real applications. I'm continuing to learn — right now that means going deeper into model evaluation, feature engineering, and starting to explore NLP and deployment.
